## Friction map (ranked by how many personas hit it)

| # | Issue | Hit by | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delete is instant: no confirm, no undo, no "deleted" message | Rio, Nell, Cara, Bea, Hal | med | By design; 5 of 11 hit it |

| 7 | **Focus lost** after done, delete or edit (list re-renders, focus goes to `<body>`); after Add via button focus stays on Add | Bea, Pia | high | **Confirmed** (no focus handling in `app.js`/`render.js`) |

**Worked well (most personas):** add with Enter, search as you type (`#tag` too), filters combine correctly,
data survives reloads, overdue tasks stand out, every control has an accessible name, Esc clears search.

## Proposed tickets (not filed; filing needs the author's OK)

1. **Prevent duplicate tasks on fast double-submit**: clear the form before saving, ignore a submit while one is in flight. (#6)
2. **Keep focus and announce changes**: return focus after done, delete and edit; add a polite live region ("Task added/deleted"). (#7, #8)
3. **Show when filters hide tasks**: after adding a task the filters hide, say so ("Added - hidden by filters, show all"); add "Clear all filters"; keep the count visible at 0. (#3, #4, #11)
4. **Edit the title** in the inline editor. (#2)
5. **Undo for delete**: a short "Deleted - Undo" message instead of a confirm dialog. (#1)
6. **Contrast fixes**: darker Delete red and count grey; rem-based font sizes; a non-colour cue for pressed filters. (#9)
